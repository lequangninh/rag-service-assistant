# BrightClean Services – Service Area and Opening Hours

## Service Zones

BrightClean serves the city of Riverton and nearby towns, split into two zones.

- **Zone A (no travel fee):** Riverton city center, Northside, Eastgate and Old Mill.
- **Zone B ($15 travel fee per visit):** Lakeview, Pine Hills, Westbrook and Cedar Falls.

We do not serve addresses outside these areas. For addresses near the border of Zone B, contact us and we will check if we can make an exception.

## Cleaning Hours

Home cleanings take place Monday to Saturday between 8 AM and 6 PM. Office cleanings take place Monday to Friday after 6 PM, or on Saturdays between 8 AM and 4 PM. There are no cleanings on Sundays or public holidays.

## Office Hours and Contact

Our customer support team answers phone calls and messages Monday to Friday from 8 AM to 5 PM. Messages sent outside office hours are answered on the next working day.
