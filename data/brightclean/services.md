# BrightClean Services – What We Offer

BrightClean Services is a residential and commercial cleaning company. All cleanings are done by trained, background-checked staff who bring their own supplies and equipment unless the customer asks otherwise.

## Standard Cleaning

A Standard Cleaning keeps a home tidy on a regular basis. It includes:

- Dusting all reachable surfaces, shelves and furniture
- Vacuuming carpets and rugs, mopping hard floors
- Cleaning kitchen countertops, the outside of appliances and the sink
- Cleaning and disinfecting toilets, showers, bathtubs and bathroom sinks
- Emptying trash bins and replacing liners
- Making beds (linens are changed only if fresh linens are left out)

A Standard Cleaning does not include the inside of the oven, the inside of the fridge, interior windows or walls. These are available as add-ons.

## Deep Cleaning

A Deep Cleaning is recommended for the first visit or when a home has not been professionally cleaned for more than two months. It includes everything in a Standard Cleaning, plus:

- Baseboards, door frames and light switches wiped by hand
- Inside of the microwave
- Kitchen cabinet fronts degreased
- Limescale removal in showers and on faucets
- Ceiling fans and vents dusted

A Deep Cleaning usually takes 1.5 to 2 times as long as a Standard Cleaning.

## Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning

For empty homes only. Includes everything in a Deep Cleaning, plus the inside of all cabinets and drawers, the inside of the oven and the inside of the fridge. The home must be empty of furniture and personal belongings, and water and electricity must be connected.

## Office Cleaning

Office Cleaning is available for offices up to 5,000 square feet. It can be booked weekly or several times per week, in the evenings (after 6 PM) or on Saturdays. It includes desks (only if cleared), kitchens, restrooms, floors and trash. Office Cleaning requires a free on-site walkthrough before the first visit.

## Services We Do Not Offer

BrightClean does not offer exterior window washing above the ground floor, carpet shampooing, mold removal, biohazard cleanup, pest control or laundry. We are happy to recommend trusted partners for these services.
