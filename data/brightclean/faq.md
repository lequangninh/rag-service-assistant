# BrightClean Services – Frequently Asked Questions

## Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No. Our teams bring all supplies and equipment. If you prefer that we use your own products, for example because of allergies, leave them out and tell us when booking. There is no discount for using your own supplies.

## Do you use eco-friendly products?

Yes. Our standard products are biodegradable and free of phosphates and chlorine bleach. Bleach is only used if the customer explicitly asks for it.

## Can I be away during the cleaning?

Yes. Most customers are not home. You can leave a key in a lockbox or share a door code. See the booking document for how we handle keys.

## What about pets?

Pets are welcome. Please tell us about pets when booking. For safety, aggressive dogs should be kept in a separate room. We do not clean litter boxes or pet cages.

## Are your cleaners insured and background-checked?

Yes. Every cleaner passes a criminal background check before starting. BrightClean carries $2 million in general liability insurance and is bonded.

## What if something is damaged?

Report any damage within 48 hours of the cleaning. We will repair or replace the item, or reimburse its fair value, up to the limits of our insurance. Items worth more than $500 that are fragile (for example art or antiques) should be pointed out before the cleaning starts.

## What is your satisfaction guarantee?

If you are not happy with an area we cleaned, tell us within 24 hours and we will come back to re-clean that area for free. If you are still not satisfied after the re-clean, we refund the price of the visit.

## Should I tip the cleaners?

Tipping is optional and always goes 100% to the cleaning team. You can add a tip when paying by card.

## How many cleaners will come?

Usually two cleaners for homes up to 3 bedrooms and three cleaners for larger homes and Deep Cleanings. The number of cleaners does not change the price.
