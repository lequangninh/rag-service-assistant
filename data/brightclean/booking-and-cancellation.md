# BrightClean Services – Booking, Rescheduling and Cancellation

## How to Book

Cleanings can be booked online on our website or by phone during office hours. New customers need to book at least 48 hours in advance. Existing customers can book up to 24 hours in advance, depending on availability.

## Arrival Window

We give a two-hour arrival window, for example 9–11 AM. The team will text the customer when they are on their way. If the team is running more than 30 minutes past the end of the window, the customer gets $20 off the visit.

## Rescheduling

A cleaning can be rescheduled free of charge up to 48 hours before the start of the arrival window. Rescheduling with less than 48 hours' notice is treated as a late cancellation.

## Cancellation Fees

- More than 48 hours before the arrival window: free
- Between 24 and 48 hours before: 50% of the visit price
- Less than 24 hours before, or no-show: 100% of the visit price

## Lockouts

If the team cannot get into the home at the booked time (for example, nobody is home and no key or code was provided), the team waits 20 minutes and tries to call the customer. After that, the visit counts as a lockout and is charged at 100% of the visit price.

## Keys and Access

Customers can be home, leave a key in a lockbox, or share a door code. Keys are stored in a locked safe at our office and are labeled with a code, never with the customer's address.
