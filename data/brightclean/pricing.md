# BrightClean Services – Pricing

All prices are in US dollars and include cleaning supplies and equipment. Prices are per visit unless stated otherwise. Sales tax is not included.

## Home Cleaning Prices

| Home size | Standard Cleaning | Deep Cleaning | Move-In / Move-Out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Studio or 1 bedroom | $110 | $190 | $240 |
| 2 bedrooms | $140 | $240 | $300 |
| 3 bedrooms | $175 | $300 | $370 |
| 4 bedrooms | $210 | $360 | $440 |
| 5+ bedrooms | Custom quote | Custom quote | Custom quote |

Prices assume one bathroom per bedroom. Each additional bathroom costs $25 (Standard) or $40 (Deep and Move-In / Move-Out).

## Office Cleaning Prices

Office Cleaning is priced at $0.12 per square foot per visit, with a minimum of $150 per visit. The exact price is confirmed after the free on-site walkthrough.

## Add-Ons

- Inside of the oven: $35
- Inside of the fridge: $35
- Interior windows (per room): $15
- Inside of kitchen cabinets (only if empty): $45
- Laundry folding (one load, already washed): $20

## Recurring Discounts

Customers who book recurring Standard Cleanings get a discount on every visit:

- Weekly: 15% off
- Every two weeks: 10% off
- Monthly: 5% off

Recurring discounts do not apply to Deep Cleaning, Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning, Office Cleaning or add-ons. The discount starts from the second visit; the first visit is charged at the full price.

## Minimum Charge and Travel Fees

The minimum charge for any home visit is $110. Homes in Zone A have no travel fee. Homes in Zone B have a travel fee of $15 per visit. See the service area document for zones.

## Payment

Payment is taken by credit or debit card after the cleaning is finished. We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express. We do not accept cash or checks. Invoices for Office Cleaning are sent monthly and are due within 14 days.
